Highlights for Gateways 2026 Annual Conference

The Gateways conference series is hosted each year at a location in the US. Gateways 2026 is excited to be coming to Washington, D.C.! Join us as we continue to bridge the connection between science and gateways. Gateways 2026 will have a robust program, open access proceedings, and a special journal publication opportunity for all accepted paper submissions. 

Join us, Wednesday, September 23 - Friday, September 25, 2026, in Washington D.C.

Conference Highlights

Join us in Washington, DC for an exciting program featuring world-class speakers, interactive sessions, and deep dives into the future of science gateways. Here are some of the sessions we are most excited about:

Keynote: Genomics at Scale with Michael Schatz

Michael Schatz, Bloomberg Distinguished Professor of Computer Science and Biology at Johns Hopkins University, will deliver a keynote on the intersection of genomics, big data, and science gateways. As a Program Director for both the Galaxy Project and the NHGRI AnVIL platform, Dr. Schatz has been at the forefront of building open-source, cloud-based ecosystems that democratize access to high-powered genomic analysis.

In recognition of his leadership, including co-leading the team that sequenced the first complete human genome, Time magazine named him one of the 100 Most Influential People in the World in 2022. His work exemplifies how gateways can empower researchers to tackle the most complex challenges in the life sciences.

Keynote: Rob Cheung

Rob Cheung is founder and CEO of Zo Computer, where he’s focused on making cloud computing accessible and usable by everyone, not just experts in the software industry. Zo Computers are cloud-native computing environments that scale horizontally and vertically. Users communicate to these computing environments in natural language, offering an unprecedented amount of information processing capacity behind a radically accessible user interface.

He will be delivering a keynote on how the process of science is changing in an era where everyone has instant access to elastic compute and AI.

Panel: The Future of Research Infrastructure: A Dialogue with Funding Agencies

What does the next decade of funding look like for science gateways? This high-profile panel brings together representatives from major federal funding agencies and private foundations to discuss the evolving landscape of research infrastructure.

Panelists will share their perspectives on the critical role gateways play in the modern research ecosystem and provide insights into future priorities for sustainability, innovation, and community-driven development. This is a must-attend session for anyone looking to align their projects with the strategic goals of the funding community.

Fishbowl: Community Panel

The fishbowl is a discussion format where a small group of participants sit in an inner circle and lead the conversation, while the rest of the audience listens from an outer ring. The key feature: any audience member can step into the fishbowl at any time to join the discussion, making the boundary between speakers and listeners fluid and open.

This format is a new type of session at Gateways 2026. A starting group of participants drawn from audience to kick off the conversation around the topics the community is wrestling with most: sustaining gateways in the era of AI.
